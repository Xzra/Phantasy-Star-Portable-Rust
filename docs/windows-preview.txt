Phantasy Star Portable Rust - Windows x64 solo game preview

UNREAL ENGINE 5.7.4 TRANSITION
The Unreal tab exports a fresh migration kit from your project. Or download
phantasy-star-unreal-5.7.4.zip for the ready-exported starter snapshot. Open its
PhantasyStar.uproject, then Tools / Phantasy: Import migration kit. Read MIGRATION.md.
It imports original meshes/rigs/authored clips and creates editable maps/props.
Rust gameplay, UI and saves still need Blueprint/C++ implementations. Unreal
is not installed in the build environment; native import is not end-to-end tested.

EDITOR AND SOURCE
For the full handoff, extract phantasy-star-engine-windows-x64.zip. Open
START-HERE.html, then Start-Editor.cmd. Character, Animation, Gameplay and Scene
tabs edit the shared project; Save / Ctrl+S, then Play / F6 opens a playtest.
Playtests never read or write character saves. Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo / redo.
The engine ZIP includes source/, its Build-Engine.cmd, and docs/engine-guide.md.
Project-only changes need no Rust compiler. New code features need Rust and the
Windows C++ prerequisites described in the guide. Keep the engine files together.

PLAY
Double-click phantasy-star.exe to begin. A new save opens character creation;
choose a name, head/body presets and colors in a live preview. Enter or Begin
adventure confirms; Escape cancels without creating a save. Existing profiles
keep all gear and progression with the original Hunter appearance.
No game installation
is needed. Follow the blue lights to the mission terminal and press E. Approach
and face the training target, then attack. Defeat it to reach level 2, follow the
yellow lights to the return pad, and press E to return to the lobby.

WASD: move     Shift: sprint     Space: jump     Q: lock on
Left mouse / J: weapon combo     X: step          RMB / F: equipped Photon Art
H: recovery (3 charges, up to 40 HP)     E: interact / retry after defeat
Middle mouse drag / arrows: orbit camera     Wheel: zoom     Escape: pause
Tab / I: inventory. Click a weapon or Up/Down selects it; E equips it.
Click a PA or use 1/2/3 to slot its disc on the selected weapon; 0 removes it.
Tab / I / Escape / Enter closes the inventory. Combat pauses while it is open.
A white/cyan cursor appears above inventory and pause menus for mouse selection.

LOBBY SHOP
Mira runs the gold-lit counter on the right side of the lobby. Approach and E
opens her shop. Click Buy stock / Sell items, select an item and click the labeled
trade button or press Enter. Escape leaves; Tab / I opens inventory.
New characters start with 300 Meseta; clearing all three trial waves awards 300.
Weapon prices: Training Sword 60, Hunter Saber 180, Photon Edge 320.
PA prices: Sweep 120, Cleave 160, Thrust 100. Selling returns half the buy price.
Purchased weapons have empty PA slots. Selling a weapon returns its PA to the
bag; selling a PA clears its slot. Equipped weapons cannot be sold. Duplicate
owned PA discs, full bags and insufficient funds are rejected without a charge.
Currency, purchases and equipment now autosave between launches.

CHARACTER SAVES
Character identity/appearance, levels, XP, Meseta, weapons, PA discs and equipment autosave.
F5 or Save progress in the pause menu saves immediately. Quit and window close
also save. Reopening places your character in the lobby with full HP and PP;
an interrupted trial starts over, without replaying rewards. Return to lobby /
keep gear replaces the old New session reset. The level cap is 100. Version 1 saves migrate on loading;
new writes use version 2 with validated appearance.
Windows save: %LOCALAPPDATA%\PhantasyStarPortableRust\profile.json
A previous version is retained as profile.json.bak. Invalid saves are preserved
and saving is disabled for that run; the pause menu shows save status.

PRESENTATION
The lobby has segmented deck plates and structural scenery. The forest has
faceted canopies, low foliage and a continuous dirt clearing. Interface panels
use a bundled Liberation Sans font; its license is in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md
and assets/fonts/OFL.txt. No installed fonts or game assets are required.

INVENTORY
Three owned swords start in your 32-item bag: Training Sword (ATK +0, cyan),
Hunter Saber (ATK +3, gold), and Photon Edge (ATK +6, violet). Each remembers its
own PA slot, initially Sweep, Cleave, and Thrust respectively. All three PA discs
are owned. Assigning a disc moves it from the previous weapon; displaced or
removed discs return to the bag. The HUD shows the equipped weapon and attack
power. Gear bonuses affect normal attacks and Photon Arts.

ADDITIONAL WEAPON CLASSES
Buy Recruit Partisan (180 Meseta) and Assault Thrust disc (100) from Mira for
long, narrow forward thrusts. Buy Recruit Knuckles (160) and Meteor Fist disc
(80) for fast close-range jab/cross/uppercut combos. Each adds +2 ATK and has
its own original weapon mesh and animations. Weapons start with empty PA slots.
Open inventory, select the new weapon, E equips it; click its compatible PA or
press 1 to assign it. Only compatible owned discs appear for that weapon.
Sword arts cannot be assigned to Partisans or Knuckles. Existing saves carry on
with their current gear; new classes are optional shop purchases.

PHOTON ARTS
Photon Sweep: 35 PP, spinning cut, hits around you.
Rising Cleave: 30 PP, heavy upward cut, strongest close-range hit.
Piercing Thrust: 20 PP, quick narrow sword attack with extended reach.
Assault Thrust: 25 PP, long narrow Partisan thrust.
Meteor Fist: 25 PP, heavy close-range Knuckles punch.
Equip one compatible art to each weapon's RMB slot. The HUD shows the active weapon's art
and PP cost. An empty slot or insufficient PP cannot activate an art. These are
original prototype skills. Equipment choices autosave between launches.

Time a fresh normal-attack press when the ring turns orange for a stronger
follow-up. Holding repeats untimed attacks. Normal hits restore PP. Avoid the
enemy's ground wedge: it chases, winds up, lunges and recovers. Defeat allows E
to retry from wave 1. Clear three waves for XP and a scored results panel.
The final enemy is faster. Two-second wave breaks restore 25 PP at the next spawn.
Return through the pad, then E at the lobby terminal deploys another trial while
keeping your level for the current session.

For native Dagan models and motion, extract the ZIP and run Play-Dagan.cmd.
It downloads/verifies both archives described below and starts the game. The
standalone EXE instead works with the original Hunter and animated Forest Crawler.

The field is an original forest clearing. The Hunter has beveled armor and a
17-bone rig with distinct combo and photon clips. Attacks now have stronger
anticipation, fast contact and held follow-through; sprinting uses a forward
lean and trailing arms. The editor previews these same clips and adjusts energy. F2 toggles bones. Player and
field art remain placeholders; this is a Classic-inspired prototype with
original balance, not an exact PSO2 recreation. Character progression is saved.
Item drops, multiple enemies and multiplayer are not implemented yet.

MODEL VIEWER
Double-click model-viewer.exe. The original sample crystal is embedded; no game
installation, Rust toolchain, or separate sample files are needed to view it.
A working graphics driver with OpenGL support is required.

VIEW A REAL PSO2 MODEL
Extract the ZIP to a writable folder and double-click Load-Dagan.cmd. It downloads
the verified Classic Dagan model archive (about 706 KB) from the official PSO2 patch
server into local\dagan.ice, checks its SHA-256, and opens it in the viewer.
The helper also downloads/verifies dagan-motion.ice (about 1.87 MiB) for native
animation. Without that motion archive, supported Dagan rigs use basic
procedural joint animations rather than standing still. The enemy HUD shows
which motion set is active. Internet access is needed for the initial download. The model is not bundled
with the release. If the archived server URL is retired, open your own supported
model file instead. Existing cached data is reused only if its checksum matches.

OPEN YOUR OWN MODEL
In PowerShell, from the extracted ZIP directory:
  .\model-viewer.exe "C:\path\to\model.obj"
  .\model-viewer.exe "C:\path\to\model.obj" --texture "C:\path\to\diffuse.png"
  .\model-viewer.exe "C:\path\to\model.ice"
  .\model-viewer.exe "C:\path\to\model.aqp" --texture "C:\path\to\diffuse.dds"

Companion MTL files supply diffuse RGB colors. Materials and textures must be
available locally. Automatic MTL texture maps are not supported. Native Classic AQN/AQM rigging and
animation are supported; see the repository animation guide for scope.
The optional texture above applies one image to the whole model.

HEADLESS TOOLS (included in ZIP)
  .\psp-client.exe inspect assets\models\training-crystal.obj
  .\psp-client.exe motion-list local\dagan.ice local\dagan-motion.ice
  .\psp-client.exe demo
  .\psp-client.exe catalog "C:\path\to\pso2_bin\data" 100000
  .\psp-client.exe asset-search "C:\path\to\itemData.json" "Frontier Wing" --classic
  .\psp-client.exe ice-list "C:\path\to\plaintext-v4.ice"

SCOPE
This is an early solo training prototype plus a separate model viewer.
The viewer opens OBJ and limited Classic PSO2 Aqua geometry (types 0xC2A/0xC31),
including the first model/LOD in an AQP package. ICE v4 Blowfish encryption and
PRS compression are supported. Other ICE versions, Oodle, NGS/Nova geometry,
additive motion, animation inheritance flags, and full game materials are not
supported. Ordinary Classic skeletal motion is supported. A matching primary
diffuse DDS texture inside an ICE archive is selected automatically; secondary
materials and game shaders are simplified. The headless demo remains available separately. No proprietary game assets are included.

Source and development notes:
https://github.com/Xzra/Phantasy-Star-Portable-Rust/tree/feat/initial-framework

MONSTER ANIMATION
The Forest Crawler has original rigged scuttle, windup, strike, recoil and death
motion without any downloads. Dagan prefers its native motion pack, and falls
back to basic bone animation if the pack is missing. Run Play-Dagan.cmd from
the extracted ZIP for the full native set. The game checks local caches next
to the executable as well as the launch directory, supporting desktop shortcuts.
